What we do

Practical preparation for real life.

Our programs combine vocational capability, character development and lifesaving competence so participants can create value wherever they live.

01

Vocational Handcraft

Hands-on pathways including tailoring, carpentry, welding, electronics and hairdressing.

02

Emergency Response

First aid, CPR, basic life support, water rescue, fire response and disaster preparedness.

03

Moral & Character Development

Discipline, ceremonial drills, teamwork, leadership, integrity and civic responsibility.

Our approach

Training beyond the classroom.

We connect practical skills with responsibility. Participants learn how to solve problems, support others, develop livelihoods and contribute to community development.

Every trained youth can become a community asset.

NACO's vision is a skilled, disciplined and self-sufficient youth population capable of transforming communities through craftsmanship, civic responsibility and lifesaving competence.
