NACO leadership structure.

A clear organisational pathway connecting national direction with state, local government and ward-level leadership.

Leadership

National to ward level

NACO's structure is organised across four levels: National, State, Chairman Self-Reliance at the LGA level, and HCS at ward level.

01
Highest level

National

National leadership provides the organisation's overall direction and coordination.

02
State level

State

State leadership connects national direction with activities and coordination within the state.

03
LGA level

Chairman Self-Reliance (LGA)

The Chairman Self-Reliance represents the LGA-level leadership layer within the structure.

04
Ward level

HCS (Ward Level)

HCS provides the ward-level leadership link, bringing NACO's structure closer to local communities.

Leadership that reaches the community.

The structure creates a clear path from national coordination to local participation.
