Purpose before profit. Capability before dependence.

NACO bridges the gap between youth potential and practical self-reliance through manpower skills, moral training, emergency response and community development.

Who we are

A training company built around useful skills.

Normal Apprenticeship Company (NACO) is a training company that empowers youths with reliable manpower skills, especially practical handcrafts, alongside moral training and lifesaving competence.

The objective is straightforward: equip people with capabilities they can use to earn a living, support others, respond to emergencies and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Skills. Morals. Discipline. Self-Reliance.

These principles guide the way NACO approaches youth development and community service.

Practical trainingMoral developmentEmergency readinessCommunity service
M

Our Mission

To empower youths with reliable manpower skills, moral training and emergency response capabilities, enabling them to become self-reliant, productive citizens who actively contribute to community and national development.

V

Our Vision

To be a leading training company that produces a skilled, disciplined and self-sufficient youth population, transforming communities through practical craftsmanship, civic responsibility and lifesaving competence.

A

Our Aim

To turn training into useful capability: medical first aid, manpower skills, ceremonial drills, self-reliance, disaster rescue and communal labour for community development.

Our principles

What every NACO pathway should develop.

01

Competence

Participants should leave with practical abilities they can demonstrate, apply and improve.

02

Discipline, integrity, teamwork, respect and responsibility are treated as essential skills.

03

Service

Training should extend beyond individual benefit into community support and national development.
