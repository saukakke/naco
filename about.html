About Us

NACO bridges the gap between youth potential and practical self-reliance. We provide structured training in manpower skills, moral discipline, and emergency response. We raise capable, responsible, and resourceful individuals who contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

To empower youths with reliable manpower skills, moral training, and emergency response capabilities, enabling them to become self-reliant, productive citizens who actively contribute to community and national development.

Our Vision

To be a leading training company that produces a skilled, disciplined, and self-sufficient youth population, transforming communities through practical craftsmanship, civic responsibility, and lifesaving competence.

What We Do

Holistic programs that shape employable, disciplined, and community-driven youths

Vocational Handcraft

Tailoring, carpentry, welding, electronics, hairdressing – practical skills for economic independence.

Moral & Character Dev.

Ceremonial drills, discipline, and integrity training for responsible citizenship.

Emergency First Aid

First aid, CPR, basic life support, and disaster preparedness.

Water & Fire Rescue

Specialized training in drowning prevention, fire evacuation, and property salvage.

Community Development

Communal labour, self-help projects, infrastructure improvement.

Entrepreneurship Mentorship

Business planning, financial literacy, startup guidance for self-reliance.

Our Services

Classified into three major pillars for targeted empowerment

Group 1: Life Saving & Emergency Response

  • Medical First Aid Training – Certification in CPR, wound care, basic life support
  • Water & Fire Disaster Rescue – Drowning prevention, fire evacuation, property salvage
  • Community First Responder Program – Trained local responders for emergencies

Group 2: Manpower & Vocational Skills

  • Manpower Skills Acquisition – Tailoring, carpentry, welding, electronics, hairdressing
  • Self-Reliance & Entrepreneurship – Business planning, financial literacy, startup mentorship

Group 3: Moral & Community Development

  • Ceremonial Drills & Discipline – Teamwork, leadership, respect, coordination
  • Communal Labour & Community Development – Infrastructure projects, public space improvement
  • Youth Mentorship & Counseling – Personal guidance and career support

What is NACO?

Normal Apprenticeship Company (NACO) is a training company that empowers youths with reliable manpower skills (hand crafts) and moral training for self-reliance, for the betterment of the community and the nation.

Aims of NACO

  • Medical First Aid
  • Manpower Skills
  • Ceremonial Drills
  • Self Reliance
  • Rescue of life and property in water and fire disaster
  • Communal labour for community development and self help
