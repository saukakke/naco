Home / Programs

Training designed for capability.

Three major service groups bring practical skills, emergency readiness, discipline and community development together.

01 / Life saving

Life Saving & Emergency Response

Build the confidence and knowledge needed to respond effectively when lives or property are at risk.

FA

Medical First Aid

First aid, CPR, wound care and basic life support fundamentals.

WR

Water & Fire Rescue

Drowning prevention, fire evacuation, rescue awareness and property salvage.

FR

Community First Responder

Prepared local responders who can support communities during emergencies.

02 / Manpower

Manpower & Vocational Skills

Practical craft pathways and entrepreneurial guidance designed for economic independence.

VC

Vocational Handcraft

Tailoring, carpentry, welding, electronics, hairdressing and other practical trades.

EN

Entrepreneurship

Business planning, financial literacy, startup guidance and self-reliance mentorship.

SK

Skills Development

Structured practice that turns learning into demonstrable, useful capability.

03 / Community

Moral & Community Development

Character and civic responsibility are integrated into the NACO training experience.

DR

Ceremonial Drills

Teamwork, leadership, respect, coordination, discipline and confidence.

CL

Communal Labour

Self-help activities and infrastructure or public-space improvement projects.

YM

Youth Mentorship

Personal guidance, career support and responsible decision-making.
