Home / Impact

Training that should show up in the community.

NACO's impact model connects personal capability with livelihoods, emergency readiness, leadership and practical community service.

Impact model

From learning to contribution.

01 / LEARN

Acquire a useful skill

Participants build practical competence through vocational, emergency-response and character-development activities.

02 / APPLY

Practice in real situations

Hands-on learning, drills, exercises and community activities reinforce the knowledge gained during training.

03 / EARN

Build self-reliance

Vocational skills and entrepreneurship guidance help participants pursue productive livelihoods.

04 / SERVE

Strengthen the community

Communal labour, first-response capability and responsible leadership turn individual growth into community value.

01

Economic capability

Practical crafts and entrepreneurship create pathways toward self-reliance and productive citizenship.

02

Emergency readiness

First aid, rescue awareness and preparedness strengthen the ability to act when emergencies occur.

03

Civic responsibility

Discipline, leadership and communal labour encourage participants to contribute beyond themselves.
